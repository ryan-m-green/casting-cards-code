Who does what, and where

Every workflow below already has a room. Read the table as the whole shell, not just the bottom row, so nothing is asked to hold two jobs.

AreaWorkflowGM doesPlayer does
bottom-left
Encounters		 Plan, build and run a fight Sees the collection of encounters. Creates, edits and deletes them. Builds groups from the card picker, rolls and commits initiative for every place at once, drags the ring into order, advances the turn, hands out conditions and hit points, ends the combat. One running fight at a time. Watches the live ring: the order, the lit place, who is down. Taps a spot to open a private note on that distinct cast card. Three verbs that fit the room: jot the moment down, open their own card, ask the DM a question.
bottom-middle
Field Notes		 Remember, study, write up Storyline today. The DM's own trophy tally (campaign_trophies, one row per distinct card put down) belongs on the same wall in bottom-right, so both roles meet the same shape of screen. Every distinct cast card the party has met: how many, the fight it was met in, which are down, and this player's own note on each. Reference while a fight runs, writing material afterwards.
middle-middle Look a card up The deck and its rolodex navigation. The same deck, whittled to what the party knows.
middle-right Be someone Soundtracks and ambiances. My character: the card, motivations, secrets. A player's own conditions and hit points live here, not in the fight room.
top-left / top-right Capture and the party Quicknotes are the DM's scratch pad. The quicknote queue and the party roster.

What the player can already do in Encounters

  • See the live ring read only, with initiative places, the lit turn, stacked groups and defeated cards greyed. built
  • Tap a spot to write a private note on a distinct cast card. Party cards are skipped by design. built
  • See their own note flags on the ring and a tally of how many notes they hold. built

What else belongs on that screen

  • The turn banner. Round and turn, plus whose place is lit. Costs nothing: currentSpotId and the spots are already in the payload. add
  • Jot it down. A quicknote opened with this encounter pre-linked, so the moment lands in the queue and can go to the chronicles later. The queue, the card linker and the chronicle mapper all exist. add
  • My card. A door to middle-right, so checking your own hit points or conditions mid-fight never becomes a hunt. add
  • Ask the DM. A line to the DM about the fight, reusing the whisper row the secrets drawer already draws. add
  • Ready / done. A ping the DM's ring can show. Needs a SignalR event and a column; only worth it if the table plays that way. later
  • Round history. Who moved when. Needs an event log the backend does not keep yet. later
